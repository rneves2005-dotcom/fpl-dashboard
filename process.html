2 · Transfer Score
projected net xG/game · next 5 · opponent + venue weighted
xG Score = projected net xG per game over the next 5 (your attack × opponents' leakiness − your defense × opponents' threat, venue-adjusted) — one number for how ownable a team's assets are. Att = projected xG created (buy their attackers, higher=greener), Def = projected xG conceded (buy their clean sheets, lower=greener). Market = net transfers this GW (the "template" forming — green bought / red sold). Signal crosses process with sentiment: CONSENSUS good + crowd buying (safe/template) · EDGE good + crowd NOT on it (front-run) · TRAP weak + crowd buying (fade) · EXODUS weak + sold.
3 · Process vs Results
xG-difference = true quality · Luck = GD − xGD
xGD ranks teams by real quality (attack xGF minus defense xGA). Luck = actual GD − xGD: green / negative = results behind process → unlucky, regress UP → BUY; red / positive = results ahead of process → riding variance, regress DOWN → FADE. L5 xGD = last-5 momentum. Click any header to sort.
4 · Fixture xG-Projection
next 5 · opponent quality, venue-adjusted
For each team's next 5 games: Att-ease = how leaky the defenses they'll face (opponent xGA/game). Higher = greener = buy their ATTACKERS. Def-ease = how weak the attacks they'll face (opponent xGF/game). Lower = greener = buy their DEFENDERS / clean sheets. Each opponent nudged ±8% for home/away. Opponent strength uses season per-game xG (toggle above).
